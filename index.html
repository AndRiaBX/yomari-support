Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No — you can play against bots straight away. An account (name, e-mail, password) is what carries your coins, stats and friends across devices, and what lets you recover the account if you forget the password.

Why do you ask for an e-mail address?

For account recovery only. We send a six-digit code to confirm the address and to reset a forgotten password. We do not send marketing e-mail.

The confirmation code never arrived.

Check your spam folder, then use “ხელახლა გაგზავნა” (resend) in the app — available again after a minute — or “სხვა მისამართი” to correct the address. Codes last 24 hours.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in dialog choose “პაროლი დამავიწყდა”, enter the account’s confirmed address, and set a new password with the code we send. We do not send recovery codes to unconfirmed addresses.

Is real money involved?

No. Coins are virtual and in-game only. They cannot be purchased or cashed out.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account (პროფილი → პარამეტრები → ანგარიშის წაშლა). This permanently erases the account, coins, statistics, match history and friends. A registered account is asked for its password first. If you no longer have access to the app, e-mail us at the address above.
