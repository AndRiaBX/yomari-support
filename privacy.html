YOMARI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 July 2026

YOMARI (“the app”, “we”) is a card-game app. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

In the current version, YOMARI collects no personal data and sends nothing off your device. Specifically:

  • Game progress & settings (coins, wins, match history, profile name, theme) are stored only on your device. They never leave the device and are deleted when you delete the app.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, or device identifiers.

Online multiplayer (future updates)

If online multiplayer is enabled in a future update, the app will connect to our game server to match you with other players. In that mode we would process a display name you choose and your in-game stats (for leaderboards), plus basic connection data needed to run a real-time match. We would not sell this data or use it for advertising, and this policy will be updated before any such feature ships.

Children

YOMARI is not directed at children under 13. The game includes a simulated coin economy (no real money) and is rated accordingly on the App Store.

Data deletion

Because all data is stored locally, you can remove everything by deleting the app. For any server-stored data in future online features, contact us to request deletion.
